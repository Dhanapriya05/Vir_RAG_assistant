# Anna University – Undergraduate Academic Regulations 2021
## Complete Student Knowledge Base (RAG-Ready)

> **Applicable to:** All B.E. / B.Tech. students admitted to Non-Autonomous Affiliated Colleges and University Colleges of Engineering under Anna University, Chennai from **Academic Year 2021–2022 onwards**.

---

## 1. ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

### 1.1 Regular Admission (HSC Students)
To join the first semester of B.E. / B.Tech., a student must have:
- Passed **Higher Secondary Examination (10+2)** (Academic Stream) with **Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry** as three of the four Part-III subjects.
- OR Passed Higher Secondary Examination (Vocational Stream) – Engineering/Technology vocational groups.
- OR Any equivalent examination accepted by the Syndicate of Anna University.

### 1.2 Lateral Entry Admission (Direct 3rd Semester)
**Category (i): Diploma Holders**
- Diploma in Engineering / Technology from State Board of Technical Education, Tamil Nadu (or equivalent) → eligible for 3rd Semester.

**Category (ii): B.Sc. Graduates**
- B.Sc. Degree (10+2+3) with Mathematics at B.Sc. level → eligible for 3rd Semester.
- Must additionally undergo **two extra Engineering subjects** in 3rd and 4th semesters.

## 5. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS

### Standard Requirement
- Ideal: 100% attendance expected.
- **Minimum required: 75%** (rounded to nearest integer).

**Attendance Formula:**
```
Attendance % = (Total periods attended across ALL courses) / (Periods per week per curriculum × 15) × 100
```

### Condoned Attendance (65%–74%)
Students with 65%–74% attendance may appear for the exam ONLY IF due to:
- Prolonged hospitalization / accident / specific illness (with medical certificate)
- Participation in sports events (with sports certificate)
- Certificate must be attested by Head of Institution and forwarded to COE.

### Prevented from Examination (Below 65%)
- **Cannot appear** for University end-semester examination.
- **Cannot move** to the next semester.
- Must **repeat the incomplete semester** in the next academic year.
- Period of prevention is **NOT counted as Break of Study**.

### Attendance Display
- Class Committee Chairperson displays cumulative attendance on Notice Board after every class committee meeting.

## 6. COURSE REGISTRATION

### Registration Rules
- Every student must register for **all courses** in the current semester.
- Registration is mandatory for both undergoing the course and writing the End Semester Examination.
- **Must also register for all arrear courses** – failing to do so means student cannot move to higher semester.
- **Maximum credits per semester: 36** (excluding Re-appearance (U) and Withdrawal (WD) courses).

### Dropping Courses (Semester II onwards)
- Maximum credits that can be dropped per semester: **6 credits**.
- Dropped courses must be registered in subsequent semesters when offered.
- Must be dropped **within 30 days** of commencement of academic schedule.

### Adding Extra Courses (Semesters V–VIII for Hons./Minor)
- Max additional credits per semester: **6 credits (max 2 courses)**.
- Max total registered credits including Hons./Minor: **36** (online Hons./Minor courses are above this).

### Professional Elective Choice Rules
- Can choose all PEC from one vertical or combination from multiple verticals.
- Cannot choose more than one course from the **same row in a vertical** in a semester (unless earlier course in that row cleared).
- Minimum enrollment for PEC/OEC: **10 students** (not applicable for Hons./Minor).
- At least **2 elective choices** must be offered per professional elective.

### No Reappearance for Improvement
A student who has **passed** a course **cannot reappear** in the same course for grade improvement.

## 7. EXAMINATION SYSTEM

### Evaluation Components
Each course = max **100 marks** = Internal Assessment (CIA) + University End Semester Examination (ESE).

### Mark Distribution by Course Type
| Course Type | Internal Assessment | End Semester Exam |
|---|---|---|
| Theory Courses | 40 marks | 60 marks |
| Theory with Lab Component | 50 marks | 50 marks |
| Laboratory Courses | 60 marks | 40 marks |
| Project Work | 60 marks | 40 marks |
| Industrial Training & Seminar | 100 marks (internal only) | — |
| EEC Courses (except Project Work) | 100 marks (internal only) | — |

### Timing of University Examinations
- **Odd Semesters (I, III, V, VII):** October – December
- **Even Semesters (II, IV, VI, VIII):** April – June
- Duration: **3 hours** for theory and practical university exams.

### Project Work University Examination
- Final project report evaluated by **external + internal examiner**.
- Viva-voce conducted separately for each student by: External Examiner + Project Supervisor + Internal Examiner.

## 8. INTERNAL ASSESSMENT (CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT)

### Theory Courses (40 Marks Internal)
- **Two internal assessments** per semester, each for **100 marks**.

| Component | Weightage |
|---|---|
| Written Test | 60% |
| Assignment / Case Study / Seminar / Mini Project / Experiential Learning | 40% |

- Total 200 marks → proportionately converted to **40 marks** (rounded to nearest integer).

### Laboratory Courses (60 Marks Internal)
| Component | Marks (out of 100) |
|---|---|
| Lab Observation, Record | 75 |
| Test | 25 |
- Converted to **60 marks**. At least **one test** must be held.

### Theory Courses with Lab Component (50 Marks Internal)
L-T-P based distribution:
| L | T | P | C | Assessment 1 | Assessment 2 | End Semester |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 4 | 3 | Lab (25%) | Theory (25%) | Lab only (50%) |
| 1 | 0 | 2 | 2 | Lab (25%) | Theory (25%) | Lab only (50%) |
| 2 | 0 | 2 | 3 | Theory (25%) | Lab (25%) | Theory (35%) + Lab (15%) |
| 3 | 0 | 2 | 4 | Theory (25%) | Lab (25%) | Theory (15%) + Lab (35%) |
| 2 | 0 | 4 | 4 | Theory (25%) | Lab (25%) | Theory (25%) + Lab (25%) |

### Project Work Assessment (60 Internal + 40 ESE)

**Continuous Assessment (60 marks):**
| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| Review I | 20 |
| Review II | 20 |
| Review III | 20 |

**End Semester Examination (40 marks):**
| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| Project Report | 10 (same for all group members) |
| Viva-Voce – External Examiner | 10 |
| Viva-Voce – Internal Examiner | 10 |
| Viva-Voce – Supervisor | 10 |

*For industrial projects: supervisor marks shared equally between department supervisor and industry coordinator.*

**Project Report Deadline:** Last working day of the semester. Failure to submit = FAIL in Project Work.

### Seminar / Case Study / Mini Project (100% Internal)
- Minimum **2 seminars** per semester.
| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| Seminar Paper | 40% |
| Presentation | 40% |
| Response to Questions | 20% |

### Industrial / Practical Training, Internship (100% Internal)
- Evaluated by a **three-member Departmental Committee**.
- Student submits: attendance certificate from organisation + brief training report.
- Evaluation: report + internal viva-voce.

### Display of Internal Marks
Internal marks must be **displayed by HODs within 5 days** from the last working day.

## 9. PASSING REQUIREMENTS

### Standard Passing Criteria
A student passes if:
- **Total marks (IA + ESE) ≥ 50%** of total marks for the course, **AND**
- **ESE marks ≥ 45%** of ESE marks prescribed.
(Applicable for both theory and laboratory courses including project work.)

### Failed Theory/Laboratory Courses (Non-Elective)
- Re-register and appear **only for ESE** in subsequent attempts.
- **IA marks from 1st attempt are retained** for all subsequent attempts.
- **From 3rd attempt onwards:** Declared passed if **ESE marks alone ≥ 50%** of ESE marks.

### Failed Professional Elective / Open Elective
- Option 1: Repeat the **same elective** (IA retained, 3rd attempt+ rule applies).
- Option 2: Choose a **different elective** → treated as new course (must attend classes, earn fresh IA, fulfill attendance, appear for ESE).

### Project Work – Failure Scenarios
- **Submitted report but absent in ESE** → deemed failed.
- **Attended ESE and failed** → must reappear in viva-voce **within 60 days** of results (pay exam fee).
- **Fail in subsequent viva-voce** → must redo the entire course when offered next.
- **Failed to submit project report by deadline** → failed; must re-register next semester.

### EEC Courses (Purely Internal)
- Passing requirement: **≥ 50% of IA marks only**.

## 10. GRADING SYSTEM

### Letter Grades and Grade Points
| Letter Grade | Description | Grade Points |
|---|---|---|
| O | Outstanding | 10 |
| A+ | Excellent | 9 |
| A | Very Good | 8 |
| B+ | Good | 7 |
| B | Average | 6 |
| C | Satisfactory | 5 |
| U | Re-appearance (Fail) | 0 |
| SA | Shortage of Attendance | 0 |
| WD | Withdrawal | 0 |

**Pass Grades:** O, A+, A, B+, B, C

**Special Grade Meanings:**
- **SA** – Prevented due to attendance shortage; appears ONLY in Result Sheet (NOT in Grade Sheet).
- **U** – Failed; appears in both Grade Sheet and Result Sheet.
- **WD** – Withdrew from exam; appears in both Grade Sheet and Result Sheet.

### Relative Grading (When ≥ 31 students passed)
- Applied to: Theory courses, lab-integrated theory courses, theory-integrated lab courses, all EEC except lab/Project Work.
- Uses **BOX-COX transformation method** to normalise and compute grade ranges.
- Only passing students' marks are inputted.

### Absolute Grading
Applied when:
- Passed students ≤ 30 (any course), OR
- Always for: Project Work / Internship and Laboratory Courses.

| Grade | Marks Range |
|---|---|
| O | 91 – 100 |
| A+ | 81 – 90 |
| A | 71 – 80 |
| B+ | 61 – 70 |
| B | 56 – 60 |
| C | 50 – 55 |
| U | < 50 |

## 11. GPA AND CGPA CALCULATION

### Formula
```
GPA / CGPA = Σ (Ci × GPi) / Σ Ci
```
- Ci = credits of course i
- GPi = grade points of course i
- n = courses cleared (semester = GPA; all semesters = CGPA)
- **U (fail) grades are EXCLUDED from GPA and CGPA calculations.**

### Conversion to Percentage
```
Percentage = CGPA × 10
```

### What is Included / Excluded
| Item | Included in CGPA? |
|---|---|
| Curricular courses | YES |
| Hons./Minor additional courses (if requirements met) | YES |
| Mandatory Courses | NO |
| Value Added Courses | NO |
| Induction Programme | NO |
| Failed (U grade) courses | NO (excluded) |

## 12. DEGREE CLASSIFICATION

### First Class with Distinction
ALL conditions must be met:
- Passed ALL courses in ALL semesters in the **FIRST APPEARANCE**.
- **CGPA ≥ 8.50**
- **NOT been prevented (SA)** from writing ESE in any course.
- Authorized break of study (max 1 year) is included within stipulated duration.
- WD (withdrawal) is **NOT counted as an appearance**.

| Programme | Stipulated Duration |
|---|---|
| B.E./B.Tech. Regular | Within 5 years |
| B.E./B.Tech. Sandwich | Within 6 years |
| B.E./B.Tech. Lateral Entry | Within 4 years |

### First Class
ALL conditions must be met:
- Passed all courses within stipulated duration.
- **CGPA ≥ 6.50**
- Break of study or prevention due to attendance counted within duration.

| Programme | Stipulated Duration |
|---|---|
| B.E./B.Tech. Regular | Within 5 years |
| B.E./B.Tech. Sandwich | Within 6 years |
| B.E./B.Tech. Lateral Entry | Within 4 years |

### Second Class
All students who qualify for degree (Clause 16.1) but do not meet First Class or First Class with Distinction criteria.

### Absence = Appearance for Classification
A student absent in ESE after having registered is considered to have appeared for classification purposes (except approved WD under Clause 17).

## 13. HONOURS AND MINOR DEGREES

### B.E./B.Tech. (Hons.)
**Eligibility to register (from Semester V):**
- CGPA ≥ 7.50 up to Semester III.
- All courses cleared in first attempt up to Semester III.

**Requirements for award:**
- Additional **18 credits** from Professional Electives (from more than one vertical of the same programme).
- ALL courses (curriculum + additional) passed in the **first attempt**.
- Final **CGPA ≥ 7.50**.

### B.E./B.Tech. Minor (Another Discipline)
**Eligibility to register (from Semester V):**
- CGPA ≥ 7.50 up to Semester III.

**Requirements for award:**
- Additional **18 credits** from any one vertical of another Engineering / Science / Humanities / Management discipline.
- Up to **6 credits** of 18 can be from SWAYAM-NPTEL.

### Important Hons./Minor Rules
- Student can opt for **only ONE** (Hons. OR Minor, not both).
- Must pay **additional tuition and exam fees** for extra courses.
- If decision not to opt for Hons. after completing some courses → those courses substitute Professional Electives.
- If decision not to opt for Minor → those courses substitute Open Electives.
- If more courses studied than required → higher grade courses counted for CGPA; rest printed in grade sheet but not counted.
- **Failed or SA additional courses** → NOT printed in grade sheet, NOT counted in CGPA.
- If all requirements met but deciding not to opt for Hons./Minor → submit declaration **30 days before VIII semester completion**.

## 14. INDUSTRIAL TRAINING AND INTERNSHIP

### Prescribed Industrial Training
- Undergone during **summer/winter vacation**.
- Minimum **2 weeks continuously** in one organisation.
- Attendance Certificate (signed by industry authority in University format) submitted to Head of Institution → forwarded to COE.

### Optional Internship
- At Research organisation / University / Industry (with HoI approval).
- **3 credits earned** → may drop **one Professional Elective**.
- **1 or 2 credits only** → NOT considered for degree classification.
- Internship: continuously from one organisation OR one 2-week + one 4-week from one/two organisations.

### Industrial Visit
- At least **ONE industrial visit per semester** from the **second year onwards** (Semesters III+).

---

## 15. PROJECT WORK

### Registration
- Registered in **final semester (VIII)**.
- Individual or **group of up to 4 students**.

### Supervision
- Must be under a **"qualified teacher"** (faculty with PG or Ph.D.).
- Industry/Research projects: jointly supervised by dept. faculty + industry/research expert.
- Student must meet supervisor periodically and attend **review committee meetings** (online with prior HoI approval if needed).

### Review Committee
- Constituted by Head of Institution.
- Members: Supervisor + Dept. Expert + Project Coordinator.
- For industrial projects: Supervisor + Industry Coordinator + Project Coordinator.
- **Three reviews** conducted; total marks reduced to 60.

### Semester-Long Internship as Project
A student may undergo a **semester-long internship** in industry / academic / research institution in lieu of project work.

### Start-Up as Project
- Start-up activity for product development can be done as part of project work.
- If it produces a **fully developed, tested, validated product** → can be considered in lieu of project work.
- Must submit a **start-up report** (concepts, process flow, publications, patents if any).

## 16. VALUE ADDED COURSES

- **Optional**, 2–3 credits, offered by departments with prior University approval.
- Credits are **over and above** the total credit requirement.
- Can be taken from other departments (with HoI permissions).
- Evaluated by **two internal assessments only** (100 marks total).
- Grades appear in Grade Sheet under "Value Added Courses/Internship/Industrial training".
- **NOT considered for CGPA computation**.
- Marks submitted to COE at least **one month before ESE**.

## 18. SKILL-BASED COURSES (EEC)

- Offered in **Semesters V, VI, VII** (one per semester).
- Two types: Pure Lab-Based and Theory-Integrated Lab.
- May involve a **firm/company mentor** alongside the college course coordinator.

### Evaluation
**With firm certification:**
- 20% internal assessment marks + 30% firm certification marks + 50% college coordinator evaluation.

**Without firm certification:**
- 50% firm marks + 50% college coordinator evaluation.

### Dropping PE/OE via Skill-Based Credits
| Credits Earned | PE/OE Can Be Dropped |
|---|---|
| 2 credits | 0 |
| 4 credits | 1 course (3 credits) |
| 6 credits | 2 courses (3 credits each) |

### Grade Point for Dropped PE/OE
For 1 dropped course:
```
Grade point = (2 × higher GP + 1 × lower GP) / 3
```
For 2 dropped courses:
```
Grade point of each = Average GP of all three skill-based courses
```

### Key Rules
- Not completed → NOT recorded in grade sheet.
- If PE/OE was dropped in anticipation but skill-based courses not passed → must re-enroll PE/OE.

## 20. WITHDRAWAL FROM EXAMINATIONS

### Valid Reasons for Withdrawal
- Medically unfit
- Unexpected family situations
- Approved sports events (approved by Chairman, Sports Board and HOD)

### Key Rules
- Withdrawal allowed from **only ONE semester examination during the entire degree programme**.
- Application must be made **within 10 days** after the date of the examination.
- Must be **recommended by HoI and approved by COE**.
- For validity: student must have registered for the course, fulfilled attendance, and earned CA marks.
- Special extraordinary cases may be considered beyond 10 days on merit.

### Effect of Withdrawal
- WD grade appears in both **Grade Sheet and Result Sheet**.
- **NOT counted as appearance** for First Class with Distinction.
- Student **must register and write** the exam in the subsequent semester.

### Withdrawal from Project Work
- Permitted only if student has **submitted the project report** before the deadline.
- Can appear for viva-voce within **60 days** of results (not counted as reappearance).

---

## 21. BREAK OF STUDY

### Duration
- Maximum **1 year** as a single spell, granted **only once**.
- In extraordinary situations: additional break of up to **1 more year**.

### Procedure
- Apply to **Director, Student Affairs** in advance (not later than last date of ESE registration) through HoI.
- State reasons and probable re-joining date.

### Rejoining After Break
- Governed by **Curriculum and Regulations in force at time of rejoining**.
- Must apply to **Director, Academic Courses** (through HoI) at beginning of readmitted semester for bridging courses.
- Must earn total credits ≥ total credits in the regulations in force.

### Key Impacts
- Break is **included** in the duration for degree classification.
- Total programme completion (including break) must NOT exceed maximum period (Clause 5.1).
- **No fee** during Break of Study period.

### Job/Start-Up During Study
- Must apply for authorised break of study for **1 year** before joining.
- Can only join after approval from **Director, Centre for Academic Courses** with proof.

### What is NOT Break of Study
- Prevention due to **lack of attendance** is NOT an authorised break.

## 22. PHOTOCOPY, REVALUATION AND REVIEW

### Photocopy of Answer Script
- Applicable for **theory courses only**.
- Apply through HoI to COE (payment of fee).
- Faculty member who handled the subject evaluates and recommends revaluation with marks breakup per question.

### Revaluation
- Apply through COE based on faculty recommendation.
- **NOT permitted** for practical courses and EEC courses.
- Maximum **5 subjects at a time**.
- Results communicated through HoI.

### Review
- For students **not satisfied with Revaluation**.
- Apply within prescribed date, through COE via HoI (payment of fee).
- **Only Revaluation applicants are eligible for Review.**

## 25. CLASS ADVISOR & CLASS COMMITTEE

### Class Advisor (Appointed by HOD)
- Ex-officio member and Convener of class committee.
- Acts as communication channel between HOD and students.
- Maintains student statistics.
- Monitors academic performance and attendance.
- Attends to student welfare (awards, scholarships, industrial visits).

### Class Committee
- Members: All class teachers + Minimum **4 student representatives** (2 boys, 2 girls) + Chairperson (not teaching the class).
- Constituted within **first week** of each semester.
- Meets: First meeting within first week; then **2–3 more meetings per semester**.
- **Functions:**
  - Solve classroom/lab problems.
  - Clarify regulations to students.
  - Inform about academic schedule, assessment dates, syllabus coverage.
  - Inform about assessment weightages.
  - Analyze student performance; address issues.
  - Identify slow-learners and arrange additional help.
- Minutes submitted to HoI within **2 days**; circulated to students and teachers.
- **Cumulative attendance displayed on Notice Board after every meeting**.

## 27. ELIGIBILITY FOR AWARD OF DEGREE

ALL of the following must be satisfied:
1. Earned the **required total credits** within stipulated time.
2. Passed all subjects (appeared for ESE and passed all courses within maximum period).
3. Passed any additional **bridging courses** prescribed (if readmitted under R-2021 from earlier regulations).
4. Successfully completed **NCC / NSS / NSO / YRC / Science Club / Literary Club / Fine Arts Club**.
5. **No disciplinary action pending**.
6. Degree award approved by the **Syndicate of Anna University**.
